Hello! I'm Ann, a postdoctoral researcher currently based at Trinity College Dublin in Dublin, Ireland. I completed my PhD at the University of Strathclyde, Glasgow back in January 2026.  

My current research interests mainly lie in numerical linear algebra including: approximating the inverse of symmetric positive definite matrices, preconditioning, and solving linear systems. Currently, my postdoc work focuses on characterising the behaviour of GMRES for block tridiagonal Toeplitz matrices with tridiagonal Toeplitz blocks. 


Some of you may recognise my as Spud from my online maths communication on various places of the internet!
